TEKNOSVERSENY / TURTLE RACE

Magyar:
1. Csomagold ki a teljes TurtleRace-Windows.zip fajlt.
2. Nyisd meg a TurtleRace mappat, es inditsd el a TurtleRace.exe fajlt.
3. Valassz teknost, majd kattints a Verseny inditasa gombra.

Kulon Java-telepites nem szukseges. Az exe mellett az app es runtime
mappakra is szukseg van: mindig a teljes TurtleRace mappat masold at.
Ez egy hordozhato Windows-alkalmazas, nem telepito.

English:
1. Extract the entire TurtleRace-Windows.zip archive.
2. Open the TurtleRace folder and launch TurtleRace.exe.
3. Choose a turtle, then click Verseny indítása to start the race.

No separate Java installation is required. Keep the app and runtime
folders next to the executable; always copy the entire TurtleRace folder.
This is a portable Windows application, not an installer.
